COMMUNITY EXPECTATIONS

Community guidelines

ZuzuCord is an independent social messaging project. It is not affiliated with Discord Inc. Registration is intended for people aged 13 or older, subject to applicable law.

Be respectful

Do not bully, harass, threaten, impersonate, or target other people. Do not share another person's private information without their permission.

Keep the platform safe

Do not post illegal content, spam, malware, or content exploiting minors. Follow applicable laws. Moderators may restrict accounts for abuse; restrictions are recorded as ZuzuCord-specific moderation actions.

Account safety

Protect your password. Email-free accounts do not support email recovery in the current version. Do not share passwords or tokens with others.

Preview status

ZuzuCord V2 is preview software. Functionality may change, and successful compilation does not guarantee every feature is free of bugs. These community rules are a project summary rather than a finalized legal agreement.
